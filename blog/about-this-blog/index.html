About This Blog

August 11, 2026

This blog will collect short notes on physics, mathematics, computation, and education. It is intended as a place to organize ideas developed through coursework, reading, and early research practice.

The emphasis will be on clear questions, careful reasoning, and concise exposition. Future entries may include reading notes, derivations, computational experiments, and reflections on physics education.
