About Me

I am an undergraduate student in Physics at Qingdao University. My early interests in programming and robotics gradually gave way to a fascination with mathematical physics, sparked by Mathematical Methods in Physics and deepened by the elegant structure of Dirac notation in quantum mechanics. Since then, I have found myself most drawn to questions that reveal underlying principles and unexpected connections between ideas. I remain keenly interested in quantum computation theory as well as emerging directions in engineering and technology. Beyond physics, I enjoy wandering through philosophy, education, and a little literature, and I am also an enthusiastic player of Teamfight Tactics (TFT). I am always glad to exchange ideas with people who are curious about similar things.
