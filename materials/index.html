Recommended Learning Resources

The video courses and Chinese textbooks listed below are resources I have used in my own studies. In my view, it is difficult to gain a thorough understanding of an entire subject from a Chinese textbook without a good accompanying course. I therefore mainly recommend the video courses, with the Chinese textbooks serving as supporting material.

English textbooks, by contrast, are detailed but often verbose, making them especially well suited to independent reading and self-study. In English textbooks, some essential topics are left for readers to explore in the exercises rather than covered in the main text, so working through plenty of problems is necessary; this is particularly evident in Griffiths's books. I have not personally read every recommended textbook in full. My choices draw on the mathematical physics syllabus of the S.-T. Yau College Student Mathematics Contest, recommendations in Chinese-language communities, and my own preferences.

This page is primarily for my own use. If you have found it through one of my recommendations, I hope it helps with your physics studies!

Courses

  • Theoretical Physics

    Lecture notes and courses:

    • Theoretical Physics Lecture Notes — David Tong (University of Cambridge)

    This teaching page offers a nearly complete sequence of theoretical physics courses. In my view, it has surpassed Landau and Lifshitz's ten-volume Course of Theoretical Physics in its role in theoretical physics education; its up-to-date content and exceptionally clear, thorough explanations make it one of the leading theoretical physics course collections of this century.

    Courses available:

    • Dynamics and Relativity; Classical Dynamics
    • Vector Calculus; Electromagnetism
    • Quantum Mechanics; Topics in Quantum Mechanics
    • Solid State Physics; Quantum Hall Effect
    • Fluid Mechanics; Kinetic Theory
    • Mathematical Biology
    • Statistical Physics; Statistical Field Theory
    • General Relativity; Cosmology
    • Quantum Field Theory; Gauge Theory
    • Particle Physics; The Standard Model
    • Supersymmetric Quantum Mechanics; Supersymmetric Field Theory
    • String Theory; Solitons and D-Branes

  • Linear Algebra

    Since mainstream approaches to teaching linear algebra in China often make it difficult for students to develop a clear, intuitive understanding, I believe a second systematic study of the subject is necessary. A strong foundation in linear algebra is essential for learning quantum mechanics. I would go so far as to say that almost all physical intuition in quantum mechanics comes from intuition for algebraic structures. For a visual understanding of the fundamentals, I also highly recommend 3Blue1Brown's Essence of Linear Algebra, available on YouTube and Bilibili.

    Video lectures:

    • Linear Algebra for Science Students — Si Li (Tsinghua University)

    Course reference textbook:

    • Linear Algebra: Principles and Applications — Si Li (PDF)

    In my view, the standard courses in advanced mathematics, probability and mathematical statistics taught in China, together with the physics-oriented group theory course recommended below, can give students a solid understanding and help develop mathematical intuition. The following lecture notes for mathematics majors are provided only as supplementary reading.
